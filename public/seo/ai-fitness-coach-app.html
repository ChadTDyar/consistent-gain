How AI Coaching Works in Momentum

The coach is given your current streak, how many habits you track, when you last logged and, right after you check in, today's plan: full workout, reduced workout, mobility and recovery, or rest day.

It keeps answers short and backs the lighter day when that is your plan.

What the Coach Will and Will Not Do

Ask it what to do on a low-energy day, how to ease back in after a break, or how to fit a short session into a full week. It will not diagnose anything. Pain and medical questions go to your doctor.

Showing up smarter beats pushing harder.

AI Coach vs. Personal Trainer

Personal trainer: A person who watches you move, corrects your form and builds a program for you. An AI chat does none of that.

Momentum AI Coach: Included with Premium ($7.99/mo or $69.99/yr), there whenever you open the app, and focused on one thing: helping you keep showing up.
